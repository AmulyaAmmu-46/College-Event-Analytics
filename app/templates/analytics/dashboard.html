Event Popularity Calculation Formula
Mathematical Formulation
Popularity Score = (40% × Fill Rate) + (40% × Attendance Rate) + (20% × Normalized Feedback)
Weight: 40%
Registration Fill Rate

(Valid Registrations / Event Capacity) × 100. Clamped to a max of 100%. Safely evaluated as 0 when capacity is not configured.

Weight: 40%
Attendance Rate

(Present Students / Eligible Active Registrations) × 100. Cancelled registrations are excluded. Excused remain in denominator.

Weight: 20%
Normalized Feedback

(Average Rating / 5.0) × 100. Events with no feedback yet are handled safely without distorting other metrics.
